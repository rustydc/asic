## Reading

Four devices on one clock is 20 pF of package input on the net, and an edge that meets tKHKL into 20 pF needs a drive under 20 ohm, which then rings through the absolute maximum ratings; the star of four passes at one drive value on the short trunk and nowhere on the trunk as placed, and the fly-by, whose loaded line slows to a third of its speed, passes nowhere with or without an end termination. Two per clock passes in a narrow band around 33 ohm. One clock per device passes from 33 to 50 ohm at every length on the card, with a 50 ohm total drive (the pad driver plus a series resistor) giving a reflection-free edge of 0.4 ns. So the die carries sixteen clocks (`psram_clk`, twelve more balls in the small-interface rows) and each is a series-terminated point-to-point trace.
