## Slots and the ring

10 surface-mount slots at 22 mm pitch in one folded row: the outbound cards in the even positions running away from the FPGA, the returning cards in the odd positions between them, so every hop skips one slot and passes beneath it on In2.Cu (a surface-mount slot has no pins through the board), the turn at the far end is a square U round the last slot, and the ring closes on the FPGA's east edge, which carries both its link ports like a card's die. Each hop is one 12-lane ribbon at 1.0 mm lane pitch. Lengths below are end to end: the card's out drop, the motherboard ribbon and the next card's in drop. Limit 200 mm per the link kind.
