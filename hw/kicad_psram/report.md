## Slots and the ring

10 slots at 22 mm pitch in two facing rows 12 mm apart: the ring leaves the FPGA into the first slot of row A, runs slot to slot along the row, crosses to row B at the far end and comes back to the FPGA's in-port on the edge facing that row. Each slot-to-slot hop is one 12-lane ribbon on In2.Cu at 1.0 mm lane pitch, a short run out of the pin row, a jog from the out-group to the next card's in-group and a short run in; the turn between the rows and the two FPGA hops bend square.
