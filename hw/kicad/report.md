## Escape density

ASIC package 23 mm, 20x20 balls at 1.0 mm. Per edge: 36 link signals on two columns of 18 rows (2.0 signals per mm of edge); 130 LPDDR signals on the north region (rows 1-8), which is 5.7 per mm and needs microvias or a build-up layer pair to escape, and is not routed here.
